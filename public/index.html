PRIVACY BY DESIGN

One check. Your choice.

Email and Comment Guard evaluate drafts only when you press Send or Post. Mouse Movement Lab is a separate, explicit opt-in and only processes pointer movement within its test area in your browser. No mouse coordinates are transmitted or stored. The website never sends actual messages, records background keystrokes, or diagnoses your mental state. In AI mode, draft text—not pointer motion—is shared with the configured provider when you click Send/Post.
